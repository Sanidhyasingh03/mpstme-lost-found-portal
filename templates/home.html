MPSTME Lost & Found Portal

The MPSTME Lost & Found Portal serves as the definitive digital repository for campus asset recovery. Designed specifically for our university ecosystem, this centralized utility allows students and faculty to securely log misplaced belongings, upload visual proof of found items, and cross-reference reports through a searchable, real-time database to ensure a seamless return process.
